# Office WiFi Access

## Networks Available

- **Blacksmith-Staff:** Primary network for all employees. Requires company login credentials (WPA2-Enterprise).
- **Blacksmith-Guest:** For visitors and guests. Password changes weekly and is posted at reception.

## Connecting to Blacksmith-Staff

1. Select "Blacksmith-Staff" from available networks.
2. When prompted for identity/username, enter your company email (e.g., name@blacksmithdata.com).
3. Enter your standard company password.
4. Accept the network certificate if prompted (this is expected and safe on office devices).

## Troubleshooting

**Can't see the network:**
- Confirm you are physically within office premises — the staff network does not broadcast to nearby buildings.

**Repeated authentication failures:**
- Verify your password hasn't expired.
- Forget the network on your device and reconnect from scratch to clear cached (outdated) credentials.

**Slow connection:**
- Office WiFi is prioritized for work traffic. Streaming or large downloads during peak hours (10 AM–4 PM) may be throttled.

For persistent issues, contact IT via #it-helpdesk on Slack.
