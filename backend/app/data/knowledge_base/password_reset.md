# Password Reset Policy

## Self-Service Reset

Employees can reset their own password at any time via the Blacksmith Data Identity Portal:

- **Portal:** https://identity.blacksmithdata.internal/reset
- **Method:** Enter your company email, then confirm via the one-time code sent to your registered phone number.
- **Password Requirements:** Minimum 12 characters, must include 1 uppercase letter, 1 number, and 1 symbol. Passwords cannot be reused from your last 5 passwords.

## If You're Locked Out

If self-service reset fails after 3 attempts, your account will be temporarily locked for security. To regain access:

1. Contact the IT Helpdesk via Slack (#it-helpdesk) or email helpdesk@blacksmithdata.internal.
2. Provide your employee ID for verification.
3. IT will issue a temporary password valid for 24 hours — you must set a new permanent password on first login.

## Password Expiry

All passwords expire every 90 days. You will receive an automated reminder via email 7 days before expiry.
