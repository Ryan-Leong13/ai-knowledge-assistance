# Flexible & Remote Working Policy

## Core Working Hours

Blacksmith Data operates on a hybrid-remote model.

- **Core Hours:** All team members must be online and reachable between 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM (GMT+8) on working weekdays.
- **Flexible Allowance:** Total daily working requirement is 8 hours (e.g., 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM with 1 hour lunch break).

## Work From Home (WFH) Guidelines

- **Interns:** Eligible for up to 2 days of WFH per week after completing their first 2 weeks of onboarding.
- **Full-Time Engineers:** Eligible for up to 3 days of WFH per week subject to team scheduling.
- **Communication Protocol:** Employees working remotely must update their Slack status (e.g., `:house: Working Remote`) and post a morning sync update in their team's Slack channel by 10:00 AM.

## Overtime & Leave Applications

Annual leave and sick leave requests must be submitted via the HR Portal (https://hr.blacksmithdata.internal) at least 3 days in advance for planned annual leave. Emergency sick leaves require a valid Medical Certificate (MC).
