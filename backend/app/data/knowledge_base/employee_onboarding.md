# Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Day 1

HR will send a welcome email with:
- Your official start date and reporting time
- Company email account credentials
- A checklist of documents to bring (ID, bank details for payroll, education certificates)

## Day 1

- Arrive at the office by 9:30 AM and check in at reception.
- HR will conduct a short orientation covering company policies, benefits, and Slack/tools setup.
- IT will issue your laptop and set up your accounts (email, Slack, GitHub, internal portals).
- You'll be introduced to your team and assigned a buddy/mentor for your first month.

## First Two Weeks

- Complete mandatory compliance training modules (security awareness, code of conduct) via the Learning Portal.
- Meet with your manager to review your onboarding plan and initial goals.
- Note: WFH eligibility for interns begins only after the first 2 weeks of onboarding are completed (see Flexible & Remote Working Policy).

## Access & Systems Checklist

New hires should confirm access to the following within the first week:
- Company email and Slack
- VPN (for remote work — see VPN Troubleshooting guide)
- Relevant internal tools for your role (Finance Portal, GitHub, project management tools, etc.)

If any access is missing, raise it with your manager or #it-helpdesk on Slack.
