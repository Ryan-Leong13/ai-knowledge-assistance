# Expense Claims Policy

## What's Claimable

- Business travel (flights, trains, taxis/ride-hailing for work purposes)
- Client meals and entertainment (with prior manager approval for amounts over RM200)
- Work-related software/tools not already provided by the company (requires pre-approval)
- Internet/phone reimbursement for remote employees, up to RM100/month

## How to Submit a Claim

1. Log in to the Finance Portal (https://finance.blacksmithdata.internal).
2. Create a new claim, attach itemized receipts (photos or PDF scans are accepted).
3. Select the correct expense category and add a brief business justification.
4. Submit for manager approval.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle, typically within 2–3 weeks of submission. Claims submitted after the monthly cutoff (25th of each month) roll over to the following cycle.

## Receipts & Limits

- All claims over RM50 require an itemized receipt — credit card statements alone are not sufficient.
- Claims without receipts may still be submitted with a manager's written justification but are subject to additional review.
- There is no blanket monthly claim limit, but unusually large or frequent claims may be flagged for finance review.
