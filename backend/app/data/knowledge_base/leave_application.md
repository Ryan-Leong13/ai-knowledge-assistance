# Leave Application Policy

## Types of Leave

- **Annual Leave:** Full-time employees accrue annual leave based on tenure (starting at 14 days/year). Interns are not eligible for paid annual leave.
- **Sick Leave:** Up to 14 days/year for full-time employees. Emergency sick leave requires a valid Medical Certificate (MC) submitted within 2 working days of return.
- **Compassionate Leave:** Up to 3 days for immediate family bereavement, subject to manager approval.

## How to Apply

1. Log in to the HR Portal (https://hr.blacksmithdata.internal).
2. Select "Apply for Leave," choose the leave type and dates.
3. Submit for manager approval.

**Planned annual leave** must be submitted at least 3 business days in advance. **Emergency sick leave** should be reported to your manager as early as possible (e.g., a Slack message before your normal start time), with the formal HR Portal application and MC submitted once you're able.

## Approval & Conflicts

Leave is approved on a first-come, first-served basis within a team. If multiple team members request overlapping leave that would impact coverage, your manager may ask you to adjust your dates.

## Public Holidays

Blacksmith Data observes all official public holidays for the country of employment, published annually on the HR Portal calendar.
