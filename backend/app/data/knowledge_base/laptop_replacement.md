# Laptop Replacement Policy

## Standard Refresh Cycle

Company-issued laptops are replaced on a **3-year cycle** for full-time employees. IT will proactively reach out roughly 1 month before your device is due for refresh to schedule a swap.

## Requesting Early Replacement

If your laptop is malfunctioning or no longer meets your work needs before the standard cycle:

1. Submit a request via the IT Asset Portal (https://assets.blacksmithdata.internal).
2. Include a brief description of the issue (e.g., battery failure, performance issues, physical damage).
3. IT will assess whether a repair or full replacement is appropriate — most requests are reviewed within 3 business days.

## Loaner Devices

If your laptop is being repaired or replaced, a loaner device is available for pickup at the IT desk (or shipped to remote employees) so you are not without a working machine during the transition.

## Interns

Interns are issued a laptop for the duration of their internship and must return it to the IT desk on their last working day. Laptops are not eligible for the 3-year refresh cycle for interns.

## Data & Handover

Before returning any device (end of internship, resignation, or replacement), back up personal files and ensure company data is not stored outside approved company systems. IT will wipe returned devices before reissuing.
